# Task 1: The Peg‑Sorting

<aside>
🧙‍♂️

Welcome, contender, to the Tri‑Wizard Robotics Endgame. Trial One awaits: sort enchanted pegs into their destined sockets using perception, planning, and a dash of wizardry.

### The tale so far

You face four enchanted pegs : Square, Triangle, Circle, and Hexagon; each 4-6 cm across and ~10 cm tall. A matching board of sockets awaits within the robot’s reachable realm.

- Robot: Manipulator X
- Sensors allowed: Two RGB‑D cameras
- Scene: Clean, uncluttered, distinct peg colors
- Modes: Teleoperation via keyboard allowed. Full autonomy earns greater glory.

---

### Quest objectives

1. Detect pegs by color and estimate x‑y coordinates and height (z) from depth (only use height or depth information if needed).
2. Classify shapes and match each peg to its correct board socket.
3. Plan and execute safe pick‑and‑place for each peg.
4. Share a fully reproducible repository and perform a public demo.

### Control and planning

- Grasping: Top grasp. Use pre‑grasp offset, approach along board normal, settle delay before close.
- Placement: Align orientation; center on socket centroid and lower slowly.
- Teleop fallback: Keys for approach, open/close, lift, move, place. Emergency stop bound to a single key.
